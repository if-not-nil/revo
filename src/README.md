garlic tuna pasta

1 serving, ~15min

ingredients: ~100g pasta, 1 tuna can (drained), 1 tbsp butter, 2 sliced garlic cloves, 1/2 lemon (or a splash of vinegar)

---
- boil the pasta in well-salted water for 1m less than the packet says (or pull when it still has a slight bite in the middle)
- warm the butter on medium-low and cook the garlic for 30-60s until fragrant, dont let it brown
- add tuna and break it up for a minute. then add the pasta and 3-4tbsp pasta water, and toss for about a minute until the sauce is glossy
- off the heat, add lemon and pepper. then, taste and add salt if needed. cheese on top is ok
